## Keep it yours

Name the portal what you like. The database entity names (`users`, `companies`, `recruiters`, `applicants`, `admins`) are fixed — every later chapter references them directly. The display name your portal shows in the UI is yours to set.

## Rethink impact

> Your portal is live. A company with twelve recruiters rebrands — different name, different website. Your schema has `company_name text` directly on each recruiter's row. You update twelve rows. Two weeks later the company is suspended for posting misleading jobs. Without a `companies` table, what entity do you suspend? Each recruiter individually? What do you do with the forty-three jobs those recruiters posted?
>
> What would a schema that treats the company as a first-class entity have looked like?
