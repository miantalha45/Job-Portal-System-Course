# Guidelines for this chapter

A quick frame before you start. This is a build chapter — read it, then build one small module that earns its keep on every chapter after it.

**Revise coming in.** You need only the basics: what an environment variable is and that Node exposes them on `process.env`, and `zod` from Chapter 3 (you don't need to know it deeply — you'll use the small slice this chapter shows). Nothing the course teaches from scratch is a prerequisite.

**Keep it yours.** There's nothing to tailor here beyond your own commit messages and your project's name in the repo. The *shape* is what matters and it isn't optional: one validated config module, secrets in a git-ignored `.env`, a committed `.env.example` that documents the contract.

**Daily working guidelines.** Make a clean commit by end of day — and before every commit, check that nothing secret slipped past `.gitignore`: your `.env` holds a database password and a signing secret, and both must stay out of Git. Carry one question as you build, because it's the trap this chapter exists to close: **if `JWT_SECRET` is missing in production, would you rather find out the instant the app boots — or hours later, when the first login silently runs insecurely?** Hold that thought; it's why this chapter makes the app refuse to start on bad config instead of limping along.
