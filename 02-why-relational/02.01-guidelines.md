# Guidelines for this chapter

A quick frame before the thinking starts — read it, then dive in.

**Revise coming in.** This chapter leans on SQL basics you already have: what a **table**, a **primary key**, a **foreign key**, and a **join** are. If those are rusty, skim a SQL refresher first — the course doesn't teach them from scratch, it builds on them.

**Keep it yours.** Everything here is taught with generic job-portal examples (companies, jobs, applicants, applications), but it's *your* portal — give it a name and make the decision yours.

**Daily working guidelines.** Even though there's no feature to ship today, you'll still touch your repo (you create the `learning-log/` folder and a scratch sketch), so: make a clean commit by end of day, and glance that nothing secret slipped past `.gitignore`. And carry one question with you as you read — it's the kind of downstream consequence this whole chapter exists to protect: **if a company edits a job's description tomorrow, what should an application an applicant submitted yesterday still show?** Hold that thought; it points straight at why this decision matters.
