# 74A.02 — Audit Routes and Journeys

Make a small route inventory:

| Journey | Start | Successful finish |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor | public jobs | job detail and sign-in direction |
| Applicant | sign-in | profile, apply, and tracking |
| Recruiter | sign-in | job management and candidate workflow |
| Admin | sign-in | moderation action |

For every route, identify its required role, data source, primary action, and the appropriate empty/error/not-found state. This inventory determines the tests and manual checks that follow.
