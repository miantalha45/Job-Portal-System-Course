# 74A.04 — Add Not-Found and Forbidden Experiences

Use route-level not-found handling for missing public jobs and internal records. Use a separate permission-safe outcome when a signed-in person reaches a role-restricted area.

Neither page should reveal whether a private record exists. Give visitors a clear exit: public jobs, their own dashboard, or sign-in as appropriate.
