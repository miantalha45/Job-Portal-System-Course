## Rethink impact

> An applicant shortlists 5 jobs: 3 at Company A, 2 at Company B. Your schema stores one application *per company* — so you create one record for Company A and one for Company B.
>
> Company A's recruiter opens their inbox. They see the applicant's answers — but the screening questions are different per job, not per company. Which job's questions do those answers belong to? Company A posted a backend role and a data role, each with completely different questions.
>
> And what stops Company A's recruiter from accidentally seeing the Company B record, since it's the same applicant?
>
> What's the right unit for an application?
