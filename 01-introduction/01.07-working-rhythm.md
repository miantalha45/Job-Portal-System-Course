# Your working rhythm

Set these habits from Day 1 — you'll be reminded of them in passing as the weeks go on, not nagged about them every chapter:

- **Commit every day**, in small, meaningful commits — and use your job portal's own name in the repo and its history from the first commit.
- **Keep secrets out of Git** — `.env` is git-ignored from the very first chapter, with a committed `.env.example` so the shape is shared but the values aren't.
- **Read the error and the docs before reaching for help**, and when you ask, lead with what you expected vs what actually happened.
