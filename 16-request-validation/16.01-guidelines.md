# Guidelines — Chapter 16

## Before you start

Two things must be in place:

- [Chapter 15](../15-validation-concept/) — the boundary contract and why validation belongs at the route layer, not the service layer.
- [Chapter 4](../04-project-bootstrap/) — the folder structure. Each feature module has a `*.schema.ts` file. That is where this chapter's schemas live.

The validation utility built here (`src/shared/validate.ts`) is used in every route handler built from Chapter 19 onwards. Get it right once.

The next chapter, [Chapter 17](../17-errors-and-status-codes/), builds the error handler that catches `ValidationError` and formats it into a consistent API response. Chapter 16 throws the error; Chapter 17 shapes what the caller sees.

## Rethink impact

> A recruiter submits a job with `{ "title": "", "screening_questions": [{ "text": "" }] }` — an empty title and a question with no text. A validation check that only throws on the first failure tells the recruiter "title is required." They fix the title and resubmit. Now they hear "question text is required." They fix that. Two round-trips for two errors that were always present.
>
> What would a better validation error look like — and what does your validation library need to support to produce it?
