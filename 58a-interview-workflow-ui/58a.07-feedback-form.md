# 58A.07 — Record Interview Feedback

Present Chapter 57’s permitted interview outcomes as a clear choice with any required notes. Explain that recording an outcome is not merely cosmetic: it changes what future tracking views can display.

Require confirmation only when it helps prevent an accidental consequential outcome, and always surface backend validation or permission failures. A completed interview should visually distinguish the recorded outcome from a pending interview.
