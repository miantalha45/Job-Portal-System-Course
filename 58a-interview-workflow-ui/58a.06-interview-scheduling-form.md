# 58A.06 — Build the Interview-Scheduling Form

Build a labelled form around the fields Chapter 56 accepts: schedule, meeting information, and notes as permitted. Describe date/time expectations in the candidate’s and recruiter’s chosen timezone; transmit the API’s required canonical time format.

On success, return to a view that visibly shows the pending interview. On validation failure, keep the values and place errors next to the relevant controls.

Do not expose a meeting link in a public route.
