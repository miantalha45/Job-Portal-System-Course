# 58A.10 — Verify the Workflow

Use separate recruiter and applicant sessions:

1. Advance one applicant through a permitted stage transition.
2. Schedule an interview and confirm it appears for both appropriate views.
3. Record an outcome and refresh both views.
4. Attempt the recruiter route as an applicant and the applicant route as a recruiter.
5. Test an empty stage, an invalid time, and a lost connection.

The UI must always converge on the server’s state after a mutation.
