# Chapter 58A — Interview Workflow UI

Chapters 55–58 define application stages, interviews, feedback, and tracking views. This chapter connects those capabilities into a coherent recruiter and applicant experience.

## Prerequisites

- [Chapter 4A — Frontend Foundations](../04a-frontend-foundations/)
- [Chapter 38 — Company Panel](../38-company-panel/)
- Chapters 55–58

Do not build a drag-and-drop board until the ordinary buttons and forms work. A stage change is a consequential server mutation: make it explicit, pending-aware, and recoverable.
