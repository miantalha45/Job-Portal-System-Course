# 43A.05 — Render Useful Job Cards

Give each result a semantic article or list item with:

- linked title and company name;
- location, work arrangement, employment type, and posted date when available;
- a short, plain-text summary;
- an unambiguous route to the detail page.

Do not make the entire card and nested buttons separate competing links. Preserve a readable heading order, and avoid relying only on color for status or metadata.
