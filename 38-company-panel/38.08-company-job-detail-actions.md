# 38.08 — Job Detail and Actions

Use the job detail page as the decision point for edit, publish, close, or other actions Chapter 36 permits. Display the current server status before offering actions and make irreversible-looking actions explicit.

After a mutation, re-fetch or revalidate the page rather than mutating an old browser copy. A rejected action should leave the confirmed status visible with a clear message.
