# 38.06 — Company Jobs List UI

Render the company-scoped job list from Chapter 37. Give every row or card a job title, lifecycle status, relevant dates, and a route to detail or edit.

Include deliberate states for no jobs, an API failure, and a pending refresh. Do not hide a closed or draft job merely because the public board would not show it—the dashboard needs the company’s full authorized view.
