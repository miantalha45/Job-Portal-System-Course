# 38.04 — Company UI Build Order

The earlier documents establish the API-backed scaffold. Build its visible pages in this order:

1. dashboard shell and role-aware navigation;
2. jobs list;
3. create/edit job form;
4. job-detail actions;
5. member management;
6. applicant pipeline link or summary after Chapter 58.

Finish one route with loading, empty, error, and success feedback before beginning the next.
