# 38.09 — Member Management UI

Render members with name or email, current company role, and only the controls the signed-in user may use. Keep invite, role-change, and removal actions separate so the reader cannot confuse their consequences.

Use a confirmation step for removal and give an empty/invitation-pending state a meaningful explanation. Server responses remain the source of truth for who can act.
