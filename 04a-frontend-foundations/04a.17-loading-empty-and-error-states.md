# Design the states around the happy path

Every data page needs at least three states: loading, empty, and error. Add a simple `loading.tsx` where a route benefits from it, an empty state that tells the reader what action to take next, and an `error.tsx` boundary with a retry option.

For example, an empty company jobs page should say that no jobs exist yet and link to “Create job”; it should not show a blank table.

Verify each state deliberately by using a new account, temporarily stopping the API, and navigating during a slow request.
