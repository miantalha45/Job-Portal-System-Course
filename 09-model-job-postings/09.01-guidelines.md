## Keep it yours

The table name (`jobs`) and the structural columns (`status`, `deadline`, `company_id`, etc.) are fixed. The contents of the JSONB `attributes` field are role-specific — you'll decide what goes there based on the kinds of roles your portal lists.

## Rethink impact

> Your `jobs` table needs to support an engineering role with `tech_stack`, `seniority`, and `remote_policy`; a sales role with `commission_plan` and `territory`; and a design role with `tools` and `portfolio_required`. You put every possible field as a nullable column.
>
> Count the columns. Count the NULLs on a typical engineering job row. Now add two new role types — how many more NULLable columns does that require?
>
> What's the better way to handle fields that only apply to some jobs?
